The finite-shot edge estimate satisfies

implies $r_t\geq\eta$. &
Relates finite-shot edge measurements to the deterministic mean-edge
condition in B1.

$N_{\mathrm{eval},t}^{\mathrm{weak}}$ includes the training and
round-evaluation operations specified in

Provides a sufficient upper bound on the cumulative boosting resource.
