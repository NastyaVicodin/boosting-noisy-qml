The original manuscript also references three supporting graphics: `figures/pqc_architecture.pdf`, `figures/circuit.png`, and `figures/class_balance.pdf`. They are included at those paths; the numerical plot script does not recreate them. They were extracted from the author-supplied compiled `Boosting_Clean_v7.pdf`, SHA-256 `0f8d3baedbd0af451b24c179df3aaa29d5f91dd1a0cb31f943b0a732cc5abadc`: paper pages 32 (architecture/Fig. 1), 48 (circuit/Fig. 9), and 51 (class balance/Fig. 10). `figures/STATIC_FIGURE_PROVENANCE.json` records how they were extracted. The original editable drawing files were unavailable. Fig. 1's gate order was checked against the executed ansatz in `src/boosting_qml/pqc/ansatz.py`; Fig. 9 depicts the separate Pauli-noise complexity argument and is not the executed numerical ansatz. The class-balance panel should be interpreted with the exact counts in `data/teachers.csv`.

The published figures and LaTeX table are rebuilt from saved records. `run_specs/` holds original run IDs and scientific settings with paths relative to this repository; `experiments/` holds the frozen code for the selected campaigns; and `src/` holds the scientific package needed by those runs and the documented checks. The publication tree omits 14 unused older orchestration and comparison files, two older plot builders, and duplicate or superseded figure exports. They remain in the separate private development checkout. The N=25 and PEC checkpoint archives and indexes under `models/` and `data/` retain selected first-hit models. Selected, interrupted-original, and PEC precontinuation scalar histories and their original-file hashes are under `records/` and `data/selected_run_record_index.csv`; `records/first_hit_proofs.zip` contains all confirmed endpoint proofs. The separately regenerated bound diagnostic vectors are under `records/bound_replay_vectors.zip` and `data/bound_replay_vector_index.csv`; reconstructed ensemble training-margin vectors are under `records/reconstructed_training_margins.zip`, with the exponential-gap decomposition in `data/bound_exponential_decomposition.csv`. These are not original saved measurement records. `PUBLICATION_RECEIPT.json` lists the included files and their hashes. For an individual replacement or first-hit proof, follow its identity and hash in the scalar row; do not treat a replay as an extra stochastic repetition.
